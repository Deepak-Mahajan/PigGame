How to Play the Dice Game

  1. Roll the Dice: Click "Roll Dice" to generate a number (1–6).
    If it’s 1, you lose your current score and your turn ends.
    Otherwise, the number is added to your current score.
  2. Hold the Score: Click "Hold" to add your current score to your total score and pass the turn.
  3. Winning the Game: The first player to reach 100 points wins!
  4. Reset Game: Click "New Game" to restart with zero scores and Player 1 starts again.

Tip: Play smart! Holding at the right time can save you from rolling a 1!
